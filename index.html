Our Mission

The Marlborough Academic Research Society (MARS) aims to provide Marlborough students from all backgrounds with the opportunity to edit, refine, and publish their research.

By facilitating robust discussions across a diverse range of academic fields, and by teaching students how to navigate the peer review process, MARS hopes to prepare Marlborough students for future scholarly research and publication.

MARS also looks to challenge and amplify existing knowledge through the dissemination of quality research in the sciences, social sciences, and humanities.
